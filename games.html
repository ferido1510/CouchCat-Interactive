Out now on Steam

A multiplayer blackjack game where the stakes are your health. Every hand is a small fight, and the table sits in a room you can actually walk around in.

  • Bet health instead of chips. Win a hand and someone at the table pays for it.
  • Mayhem cards that bend the rules, including Hot Potato, Drunkard, Spy and Gambler.
  • A free roam room. Grab a drink from the fridge, set it on the table, throw it at the dealer.
  • Lose all your health and you become a ghost who can still throw things at the living.
  • Proximity voice chat. Voices fade with distance, and ghosts hear the living but not the other way around.
