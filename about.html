About

A small studio that likes playing games with friends.

Who we are

CouchCat Interactive is an independent game studio made up of a small group of friends. We make games that are best played with other people, whether that is across a table or across a voice call.

Our first game, Blackjack Mayhem, launched on Steam in 2026.

How we work

We keep the team small and the feedback loops short. Nothing ships until we have played it with real people in real sessions, and we would rather fix one rough edge properly than add three new features.
