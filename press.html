About the studio

CouchCat Interactive is an independent game studio that makes games to be played with friends. Its first game, Blackjack Mayhem, is a multiplayer blackjack game where players bet their health, play rule-bending mayhem cards and talk over proximity voice chat. It is available now on Steam.
